# Jira and Confluence Automation Ideas

## 1. Delivery Risk Digest

**Problem it solves:** Managers often discover blocked work, slipping issues, and overloaded teams too late to intervene effectively.

**Data it needs:** Jira issue status, priority, assignee, sprint, due date, story points, labels, blockers, dependencies, and recent status changes. It can combine this with team ownership and sprint information to produce a scheduled risk summary.

## 2. Automated Weekly Project Brief

**Problem it solves:** Preparing a reliable weekly project update requires manually gathering progress, decisions, risks, and upcoming milestones from multiple Jira projects and Confluence pages.

**Data it needs:** Jira issues completed and in progress, sprint reports, epics, milestones, release dates, issue comments, and linked Confluence project updates, meeting notes, and decision records.

## 3. Decision and Action-Item Tracker

**Problem it solves:** Decisions and follow-up actions from meetings can become buried in notes, causing unclear ownership and missed deadlines.

**Data it needs:** Confluence meeting notes, decision tables, action-item text, named owners, due dates, project or team references, and Jira issue links. The automation can create or update Jira tasks and notify owners when deadlines approach.
