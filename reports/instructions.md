# How to Complete the Status Report

Start with `template.md` and save a copy for the reporting period. Replace every bracketed prompt with current, factual information, then remove any optional prompts or unused bullets before sharing.

## Report Details

- **Project:** Use the project or workstream name readers will recognize.
- **Report owner:** Name the person who prepared the update.
- **Reporting period:** Give the start and end dates in `YYYY-MM-DD to YYYY-MM-DD` format (for example, `2026-09-21 to 2026-09-25`). Include the year in both dates.
- **Overall status:** Choose one status using these guidelines:
  - **Green:** Work is on track; no significant intervention is needed.
  - **Amber:** A risk or delay may affect a commitment; mitigation or a decision is needed.
  - **Red:** A commitment is missed or likely to be missed; immediate help or a change in plan is needed.
  - Select a status only when the user provides it or project facts explicitly support it; do not infer Green from missing or undocumented risks.
  - If the available information does not establish a status, ask the user to confirm it before finalizing the report.

## Highlights

Lead with the most important outcomes from this period. Describe what changed or shipped and why it matters. Prefer specific results over activity, for example, "Completed and tested the issue status mapping" rather than "Worked on mappings." Keep this section concise.

## Delivery Updates

List the important milestones, features, or workstreams. For each, state its current status, concrete progress, and expected completion date when known. Be explicit about what is complete, in progress, or not started; avoid implying completion when work is still being verified.

## Risks and Dependencies

Include issues that could affect delivery, quality, or scope. Explain the impact, the mitigation or next action, and who owns it. For a decision request, state exactly what decision is needed, who should make it, and by when. If there are no material risks or decisions, write "None identified" instead of leaving the section blank.

## Focus for Next Week

List a small number of priorities and the outcome expected for each. Make them concrete enough that the next report can say whether they were achieved. Carry forward unresolved risks only when there is a specific next action.

## Final Check

- Confirm dates, names, statuses, and delivery claims are accurate.
- Make sure Amber or Red status is explained in Risks and Dependencies with an owner and next action.
- Remove empty bullets, unused placeholders, and internal notes.
- Keep the report brief and understandable to someone who was not in the project meetings.
